Tips that work for many shift workers

  • Keep a consistent sleep/wake time, even on days off, when you can.
  • Use blackout curtains and a white-noise fan for day sleep.
  • Avoid bright screens 30–60 minutes before bed.
  • Keep caffeine to the first half of your shift.
  • A short nap (20 minutes) before a night shift can help.
