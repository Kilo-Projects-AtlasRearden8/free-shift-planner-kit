Free Shift Planner Kit

Printable brain sheets, sleep logs, handoff templates, and weekly schedules for nurses and shift workers.

Large-print friendly. No account needed. No sign-up. Nothing you write here is saved or sent anywhere — print and fill in by hand.

Planners

  • Brain Sheet (shift report) — patient basics, vitals trend, IV access, medications, labs, to-do list, handoff summary, sign-off.
  • Sleep Log — one-week sleep tracker with shift times, sleep hours, quality, caffeine, and naps.
  • SBAR Handoff Template — Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation with vitals grid, census, and task list.
  • Weekly Schedule — large-print week-at-a-glance for shifts, sleep, and appointments.

How to use

  1. Open a planner in your browser.
  2. Print it (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P). US Letter, margins set for printing.
  3. Fill it in by hand on the unit or at home.
